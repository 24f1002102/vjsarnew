07 / Security & Data Sovereignty

Your keys. Your data. Nobody else's.

Context DMS supports a Bring Your Own Key encryption model. You provide your public key during onboarding, and documents and metadata can be encrypted at rest under that key. When AI processing is needed, content is decrypted in isolated, ephemeral compute for the duration of the task and plaintext is not intended to persist beyond processing.
